Our offerings

From hemp fields to mushroom houses to guided farm walks — everything we do starts with the soil and ends with your table.

01 — Cultivation

Organic Hemp Cultivation

Hudson's Farm cultivates certified organic industrial hemp across dedicated plots on our 6.8-hectare property. Our hemp is grown without herbicides, pesticides, or synthetic fertilisers — relying on natural compost, companion planting, and our two on-site natural dams for irrigation.

We work with licensed cultivars suited to the Magaliesberg climate, producing fibre-grade and seed-grade hemp for direct sale and processing partnerships.

  • 100% pesticide-free cultivation
  • Licensed and compliant with South African hemp regulations
  • Natural dam irrigation, minimal water footprint
  • Available for bulk enquiry and research partnerships

02 — Harvest

Gourmet Mushroom Growing

Our controlled mushroom growing environment produces oyster, shiitake, and seasonal specialty varieties harvested at peak freshness. Substrate is prepared from clean agricultural waste, completing a full farm-cycle loop.

We supply chefs, restaurants, farm-shop regulars, and home cooks who value knowing exactly where their food comes from. Harvest-to-door turnaround is typically same or next day.

  • Oyster, shiitake, and seasonal varieties
  • Chemical-free substrate from farm waste
  • Whole mushrooms and dried available
  • Batch bookings for restaurants and traders

03 — Microgreens

Microgreens Production

Our microgreens are grown in clean, controlled trays and harvested at 7–14 days for maximum nutrient density. We grow sunflower, radish, pea shoot, broccoli, and brassica blends — with seasonal rotations throughout the year.

Subscribe weekly and we'll pack your order fresh on harvest day. Minimum order is one standard tray; bulk and restaurant trays available.

  • Weekly subscription packs available
  • Sunflower, radish, pea, broccoli, and brassica blends
  • Grown without synthetic inputs
  • Pickup from the farm or local delivery (enquire)

04 — Fresh Produce

Organic Food Garden

Our food garden runs on seasonal rotation — vegetables, herbs, leafy greens, and fruit grown in living soil enriched by our own compost and bokashi systems. No synthetic inputs, ever.

Fresh harvest boxes are available for collection from the farm on scheduled days. Boxes change with the season and are packed the morning of your pickup.

  • Seasonal vegetables, herbs, and fruit
  • Fresh harvest box pickups, scheduled in advance
  • Composted, living-soil beds
  • Great for families, home cooks, and health-conscious buyers
