The Land

6.8 hectares of living soil

Nestled in the Magaliesberg valley, Hudson's Farm covers 6.8 hectares of productive, chemical-free land. Two natural dams feed the property year-round, supporting everything from our food garden to the pecan orchards that line the property's edge.

Farming here is guided by one principle: leave the soil richer than we found it. Every growing decision — from companion planting to water management — is made with the next generation in mind.

The Closed Loop

Every part of the farm feeds another

Nothing leaves the cycle. Hudson's Farm is designed so every input returns to the earth — dams feed the crops, crops feed the compost, compost feeds the soil.

Natural Dams

Two permanent on-site dams supply all irrigation — no municipal water, no pumping costs beyond the borehole that tops them up.

Living Soil

Crop residues, hemp stalks, and organic matter are composted back into the growing zones — the soil gets richer every season, not depleted.

Zero Waste

Hemp stalks return to compost. Mushroom substrate feeds the food garden. Nothing leaves the system as waste — it all becomes input for the next cycle.

Chemical-Free. Always.

No herbicides, no pesticides, no synthetic fertilisers — ever. The closed loop makes them unnecessary. Natural systems in balance don't need chemical intervention.
