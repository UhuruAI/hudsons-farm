Our Story

Organic. Regenerative.
Rooted in Africa.

Hudson's Farm is a working organic farm in the Magaliesberg valley — 6.8 hectares of living soil, two natural dams, and a simple commitment to growing food the right way.

The Farm

6.8 hectares of purpose

Hudson's Farm sits in the heart of the Magaliesberg, one of the oldest mountain ranges on earth and one of South Africa's most fertile farming regions. The farm covers 6.8 hectares of productive land, fed by two permanent natural dams and surrounded by indigenous vegetation.

We grow organic hemp, gourmet mushrooms, microgreens, and fresh seasonal produce — alongside a mature pecan orchard that lines the eastern boundary. Everything is grown without synthetic chemicals, herbicides, or pesticides.

Farming here is guided by one principle: leave the soil richer than you found it. Every decision — composting, water management, companion planting, crop rotation — is made with the next decade in mind, not the next quarter.

Our Philosophy

Regenerative, not just organic

"Organic" tells you what we don't use. "Regenerative" tells you what we're actively doing: rebuilding topsoil, restoring water cycles, increasing biodiversity, and creating a farm ecosystem that becomes more resilient with every growing season.

We use composting and bokashi systems to return nutrients to the soil. Our two natural dams are managed for minimal runoff and maximum groundwater recharge. Cover crops protect the soil between seasons. The hemp, mushrooms, and food garden all feed one another in a closed-loop system.

This isn't idealism — it's economics. A living soil grows better food, more reliably, for less input cost. That's why every rand we reinvest goes back into the land.

"Regenerative farming is farming as if the next generation matters — because they do."

— Peter Hudson, Owner, Hudson's Farm
How We Farm

Three commitments we keep

These aren't marketing claims — they're the practical decisions that shape every crop cycle at Hudson's Farm.

No synthetic inputs

No herbicides, pesticides, or synthetic fertilisers. Ever. Our soil health comes from compost, cover crops, and diversity — not from a bag.

Water stewardship

Our two natural dams are managed as living systems — not just reservoirs. Irrigation is timed and minimal. Runoff is captured and reused.

Radical transparency

Come visit. Walk the fields. Ask what's in the compost. We're an open farm — because nothing we do needs to be hidden.

The Magaliesberg

A place worth farming well

The Magaliesberg is one of the oldest mountain ranges in the world — geologists estimate the quartzite ridgeline formed roughly 2.5 billion years ago. The valley between the ridges collects rainfall, moderates temperature extremes, and supports a diversity of flora and fauna that makes it unlike anywhere else in the Highveld.

It's a privilege to farm here. The springs, the dams, the pecan trees, the rich clay-loam soil — these aren't things you create. They're inherited from the land itself. Our job is to be good stewards of what's already here.

The farm is located in the broader Magaliesburg district, within easy reach of Pretoria and Johannesburg — making it an accessible destination for day visitors, tour groups, and families looking for a genuine farm experience.

The property

6.8 hectares of organic land

Orchard
